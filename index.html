How the AI opponent works

The AI is a built-in computer opponent. It rolls the digital dice, chooses what to keep, rolls again, then chooses a category.

It is rule-based, not internet-connected. It tries to chase Yahtzee, strong multiples, straights, full house, upper-section bonus, and Chance. If there is no useful score, it crosses out a category.

Human turns still work manually. After a human submits a score or crosses out a category, the scorecard locks until End Turn / Next Player is clicked.
